Hello, my name is Emmanuel Ndogo. I am originally from Abia State in the eastern part of Nigeria but currently live in Lagos—a place I proudly call home because it is both my birthplace and the city where I grew up and developed my values. I am twenty-seven years old and currently enrolled in BYU–Pathway Worldwide at Brigham Young University, studying Web Development online. My long-term goal is to become a highly skilled full-stack developer, combining creativity with technical expertise to build innovative and meaningful digital solutions. Among all aspects of web development, I am especially drawn to the backend because of my curiosity about how systems, data, and processes work behind the scenes. My love for technology and my inquisitive nature inspire me to keep learning and experimenting. Beyond academics, I am passionate about activities that involve adventure, strategic thinking, and teamwork. I enjoy coding side projects that challenge my skills, participating in technology-based service opportunities that improve lives, and contributing to projects that have a positive social impact. In my free time, I also love reading, exploring new ideas, and watching sports, which help me stay balanced, motivated, and connected to my community.

Nigeria

Nigeria is a vibrant country located in West Africa, known for its rich cultural heritage, diverse languages, and natural beauty. It is the most populous country in Africa and has a rapidly growing technology and creative industry. From its bustling cities like Lagos and Abuja to its scenic landscapes and traditions, Nigeria is a land full of energy, opportunities, and resilience.
